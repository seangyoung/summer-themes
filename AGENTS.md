# Summer Themes Editorial And Site Guide

## Purpose

Create shareable, kid-focused themed-week plans that are enjoyable, practical, and adaptable for families. Each theme should be coherent enough to feel special, broad enough to fill a week, and appropriate for a wide age span of roughly 6 to 16.

## Planning Standards

- Develop a detailed weekly plan only after the user approves the theme.
- Start each week on Sunday and reserve Monday for a library visit whenever practical.
- Field trips may fall on any day except Sunday.
- Balance heat-conscious indoor activities with a few meaningful outdoor options. The family has access to a pool, splash pad, parks, playgrounds, and a small rocky lakeside beach area.
- Prefer free or low-cost activities for a group of 7 to 9 people. Include one or more clearly marked optional enhancements when a paid activity is especially fitting.
- Estimate per-person costs for activities, supplies, admission, parking when material, and other foreseeable fees. Date-stamp prices and link to official information or ticket pages.
- Keep historical and educational context relevant to the chosen theme. Do not add unrelated framing or commentary.

## Recommendation Standards

- Give specific, curated recommendations only. Do not include rejected options or explain what was ruled out.
- Treat books, movies, shows, and documentaries as a core part of every plan, not an afterthought.
- Link every book, movie, show, educational resource, and paid activity. Use reliable primary or established reference sources where practical.
- For every movie recommendation, include its release year and MPAA rating immediately after the title.
- Group books and screen recommendations by age range, genre, or viewing/reading purpose as suits the list. Avoid one undifferentiated long list.

## Weekly Page Standard

- Build each approved plan as a polished, mobile-friendly webpage with a clear theme overview, daily Sunday-through-Saturday plan, and linked resources.
- Include one intentional image for each day. Do not add a separate cover page or image collage.
- Name each day of the week; do not use numbered day labels.
- Each daily entry should normally include the main activity, a craft or hands-on project, food direction, entertainment or reading tie-in, an estimated cost, and indoor/outdoor context when useful.
- Use the established site visual language unless the user asks to redesign it: warm natural palette, readable expressive serif headings, clean sans-serif body text, and substantial image-led day cards.

## Publishing Workflow

- Keep new work in the local repository, verify it, then commit and push only work that the user has approved for publication.
- Update the landing page and README when a new themed week is added.
- Do not include private schedules, names, locations, or family photos in public site content.
